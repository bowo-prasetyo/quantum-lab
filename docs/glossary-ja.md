# 用語集: English → 日本語

The terms used in the Japanese translation (`locales/ja.js`,
`content/ja.js`), so every page says the same thing the same way. A
reviewer who changes a term here should change it in both files.

Style: polite です・ます form for explanations, short noun phrases for
labels and buttons, 「」 for quotations. Standard terms from Japanese
quantum computing texts; katakana where that is the usual term. Quantum
notation, gate names (H, X, CNOT…) and *Glass Qubit* are not translated.
No spaces between Japanese and numbers or symbols (3量子ビット), except
around Latin words and formulas where they help reading.
